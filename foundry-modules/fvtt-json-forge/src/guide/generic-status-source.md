# 通用状态机制验收

以下是用于本地功能验收的两只虚构怪物。

## 生物一：Veil Stalker

Medium Fey, Neutral

Armor Class 15
Hit Points 90 (12d8 + 36)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 18 (+4) DEX 14 (+2) CON 16 (+3) INT 10 (+0) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 10 (+0)

Senses passive Perception 11
Languages Common
Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)
Proficiency Bonus +3

Actions

Blinding Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target is Blinded for 1 minute. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw, ending the effect on a success.

## 生物二：Dread Stalker

Medium Fey, Neutral

Armor Class 15
Hit Points 90 (12d8 + 36)
Speed 30 ft.

STR 18 (+4) DEX 14 (+2) CON 16 (+3) INT 10 (+0) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 10 (+0)

Senses passive Perception 11
Languages Common
Challenge 5 (1,800 XP)
Proficiency Bonus +3

Actions

Dread Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d6 + 4) Slashing damage, and the target is Frightened for 2 rounds. At the end of each of its turns, the target can make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw, ending the effect on a success.
