# GRANDMOTHER SEA HAG

GRANDMOTHER SEA HAG CR 15
Glimpses of a monstrous, twisted form glimmer within
whenever the sea-witch’s illusory shroud shifts, her searing
gaze unblinking and unwavering.
AC 16 (Natural Armor) Initiative +7 (17)
HP 272 (32d8 + 128) Size Medium
Speed 30 ft., Swim 40 ft. Type Fey
MOD SAVE MOD SAVE MOD SAVE
Str 20 +5 +5 Dex 15 +2 +2 Con 19 +4 +9
Int 15 +2 +7 Wis 17 +3 +8 Cha 21 +5 +10
Skills Deception +10, Insight +8, Perception +8, Stealth +12
Senses Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 18
Languages All
Drives Keep Grudges, Punish Disrespect, Sow Mistrust
XP 13,000 Proficiency Bonus +5
Traits
Illusory Mimicry. The hag can perfectly mimic the voice
and appearance of any Beast or Humanoid that is Cursed
by or Indebted to it.
Coven Craft. While at least two allies with this trait are
within 30 feet of the hag, its Attack Rolls have Advantage
and creatures have Disadvantage on any saving throw
they make against effects it controls.
Stilling Purge. At the start of each of the hag’s turns, it can
end one spell or condition affecting it. If it does, it can’t
take Bonus Actions or Reactions until the start of its next
turn.
Actions
Multiattack. The hag makes two attacks, one of which it
can replace with a use of its Seed Terror or Steal Passion.
Rotflesh Claw. Melee Attack Roll: +10, reach 5 ft. Hit: 14
(2d8 + 5) Slashing damage plus 11 (2d10) Necrotic
damage.
Seed Terror. Illusory blight spreads from the hag in a 15-
foot Emanation, making flesh appear to sag and rot. Each
creature in the area must make a
Intelligence or Wisdom Saving Throw:
(Target’s Choice) DC 18. Failure: The creature is
Frightened for 1 minute (Save Ends at end of turn).
Steal Passion(Concentration). The hag tears a token from a
creature within 5 feet, forcing it to make a Charisma
Saving Throw: DC 18. Failure: The creature is Cursed.
Failure by 5 or More: The hag can maintain this effect
without Concentration.
While Cursed in this way, the creature suffers one of the
following effects of the hag’s choice.
The creature can’t speak louder than a whisper, and can’t
cast spells of Level 1 or above.
The creature’s Speed is reduced by 10 feet and it can’t take
Bonus Actions or Reactions.
The creature has Disadvantage on any d20 Test that uses
Strength or Charisma.
Nettletide(Level 4 Spell). Seafoam and stinging tendrils
surge from the hag in a 30-foot Cone, forcing each
creature in the area to make a
Constitution Saving Throw: DC 18. Failure: 21 (6d6)
Poison damage. Failure by 5 or More: The creature is
additionally Paralyzed until the end of its next turn.
Success: Half damage.
Bilebrine(Level 5 Spell). Stinging brine surges over a
creature the hag can see within 60 feet, forcing it to make
a Constitution Saving Throw: DC 18. Failure: 22 (5d8)
Acid damage plus 22 (5d8) Necrotic damage. Success:
Half damage.
A creature reduced to 0 Hit Points by this damage is
infected with Merthirst.
Mariner’sWoe(Recharge 5-6,Level 8 Spell). The hag calls
the sea to reject a creature within 60 feet, forcing it to
make a Charisma Saving Throw: DC 18. Failure: The
creature is Cursed. While Cursed in this way, the creature
can’t breathe underwater by any means, and sinks 15 feet
at the start of each of its turns while it is submerged.
Bonus Actions
Drowner’s Dive. The hag moves up to half its Swim Speed,
then can attempt to Hide if it has at least Half Cover.
Sargassum Grasp. A tangle of seaweed blooms to envelop
a submerged creature within 30 feet, forcing it to make a
Dexterity Saving Throw: DC 18. Failure: The creature
is Restrained until the end of its next turn (escape DC 15)
and dragged 15 feet in a direction of the hag’s choice.
Reactions
Courage to Dread. Trigger: A creature within 30 feet
succeeds on an Intelligence or Wisdom Saving Throw.
Response: The hag deals 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage to it.
Legendary Actions
The hag can take one legendary action, choosing from the
options below.
Chaos Churn. The hag moves up to its Speed, then can
use its Nettletide or Bilebrine.
Malice Deeps. An ice wave roils forth; each creature
Cursed by or Indebted to the hag within 40 feet of it takes
9 (2d8) Cold damage and is pushed up to 10 feet in a
direction of the hag’s choice.
Pilfered Spell. The sea hag casts a spell of Level 5 or below
known by a creature Cursed by or Indebted to it, using
that creature’s spell slots.
Death Glare(Only if Bloodied). One Frightened creature
within 30 feet must make a Wisdom Saving Throw: DC
18. Failure: The creature is Stunned until the end of its
next turn. Failure by 5 or More: The creature drops to 0
Hit Points.


## Referenced definitions from the same PDF

Hag Debts
A creature that has made an unfulfilled promise to a hag, or
one that removes a Curse created by a hag, becomes
Indebted to the hag.
A creature Indebted to a hag becomes psychically
connected to it. The hag can telepathically communicate
with it over any distance and can choose to perceive through
its senses at will. Once per day, the hag can force an
indebted creature to reroll d20 Test, taking the second result.

Merthirst
A creature infected with merthirst loses all appetite, its eyes
become bloodshot, and a crust of pale salt gradually
accumulates around its nose and lips. It can keep solid food
down only if washed down with sea water.
